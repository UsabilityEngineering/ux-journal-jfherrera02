# Booking a Flight
By Jose Herrera

Recently, my friends and I started making plans to go on a trip to Japan, so I began to look for flights through my credit card's travel portal. The end goal was to find the best flights available for us at the cheapest price possible, as we are university students trying to save money. 
When I first accessed the menu, I was pleased with how straightforward it was. All I had to do was select the dates, locations, and type of flight to begin my search. 

![Pic_1](/assets/booking_1.png "Pic 1")

I was then presented with an incredibly **useful** feature that presented the dates in a color coded format showcasing the varying prices of the flight to my destination. Color coding here allowed me quickly identify the most affordable travel dates without extensive searching. This was not something I would typically see in many other booking websites, so I was very happy to see this feature.

![Pic_2](/assets/booking_2.png "Pic 2")

After clicking search on my desired trip, I found myself presented with lots of information that I found to be a bit overwhelming at first, but after carefully looking into it, I thought that what was presented to me was quite **valuable**. I was given lots of information on my flight and ways that I could potentially save money or have more control over booking my flight. This information was very memorable to me, as I had never seen such depth of information and control provided anywhere else. Overall, this process had a lot of **learnability** because the entire process was easy to understand and navigate in my first interaction.

![Pic_3](/assets/booking_3.png "Pic 3")

Finally, I was given the option to choose my airline, select what kind of class I would like to ride in, and review all the information necessary for that specific flight. This entire booking process demonstrated principles of **flexibility and efficiency** because I was able to quickly, and without many issues, search for the flight I wanted, and I was even given several options to alter my flight in order to save money or have a better experience.
